# D-07 — Map-first workspace

Accepted by the Product owner in the 16 October 2026 request. D-06's persistent
right analytics and all other approved business contracts remain in force.

Desktop center contains only the map and complete fourteen-column table. The saved
context moves into the existing right inspector, with expandable saved Union/All
origins and their FIAS identities. Compare stays near the top in sticky inspector
controls. Selection context is integrated into the existing detail sections,
without a second copy of the selected name. Existing Parameters/Result openers
become compact controls over the map only at the respective drawer breakpoints.

Remove the table's calculation-methodology disclosure, its renderer and its CSS.
Keep backend `cluster_table.methodology`, mathematical definitions, periods,
canonical/legacy/Bear meanings and individual metric helps. Preserve formerly
exclusive mode influence through the existing weighted-price help; period pills,
raw-value helps, coverage and frozen context retain the other definitions.

No new control panel, replacement methodology block, dependency, backend/API,
metric formula or XLSX writer change. Critical warnings and frozen/draft status
remain in side panels; narrow Result controls flag attention without occupying
a permanent strip above the map. Native/human/production acceptance remain
separate from automated functional evidence.
